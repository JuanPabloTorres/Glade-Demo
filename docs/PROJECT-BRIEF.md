# Project Brief

## Problem
Legal teams collect the same client facts across questionnaires and documents. Differences are often discovered late, causing rework and risk.

## Demo solution
MatterReady creates one canonical matter record, extracts facts from uploaded text, surfaces conflicts instead of silently overwriting values, and shows a readiness score based on required intake fields and documents.

## Evaluation value
- Product thinking aligned with legal operations.
- Explicit data lineage.
- Human-in-the-loop conflict resolution.
- Maintainable full-stack architecture.
- Deployable without a paid AI dependency.

## Out of scope
Authentication, billing, e-signature, court filing, OCR, production PII, and legal advice.
